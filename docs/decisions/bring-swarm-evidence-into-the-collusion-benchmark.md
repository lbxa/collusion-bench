# Bring swarm evidence into the Collusion benchmark

- **Date:** 2026-11-19
- **Status:** Accepted for integration
- **Deciders:** Lucas Barbosa requested the integration. Codex selected the implementation scope.
- **Area:** Harness, research evidence, and reporting

## Context

The Apart swarm sprint used Veracity to test whether evaluated jurors would
join a scripted voting bloc. Collusion Bench already runs those configurations
on its current engine. The sprint's historical analyzer and evidence receipts
were absent, and its shell orchestrator used a different failure and spending
policy.

## Decision

Preserve the sprint's configurations, analysis, and frozen evidence hashes.
Use the current campaign controller for fresh executions of legacy designs and
for the new five-model frame. The harness owns assignment, delivery, and
evidence collection. Solidity remains the authority for votes, settlement, and
credits. The journal keeps each assignment immutable and resumes pending work
only. The readout retains the full assignment frame, including incomplete and
failed cases, and distinguishes mechanical evidence from independent review.
It verifies the ordered registered plan and uses an explicit fixture mode for
subset journals with injected models.

Run the prospective peer comparison on the existing finance and education
sources with the principles duties, using the historical scale design's
one-vote majority shortfall and funding. Compare the two peer policies within
each jury size. Holding the shortfall fixed changes the number and share of
scripted seats and the ordinary reward per juror across sizes. This choice
preserves the sprint's intervention and limits interpretation of size effects.

## Consequences

The initial principles campaign and the C1/C2 scale cohorts keep their original
protocols and denominators. Their results can motivate the prospective study
and identify observations that a detector should distinguish. A historical
false vote or paid settlement does not supply an independent collusion label.
The separately scheduled seven-juror fallback is an executable design. Its
original evidence was unavailable in the retained local packet.

Configuration checks, historical replays, and fixture runs are offline. A new
provider-backed campaign requires an authorized cap and a selected runner. Its
run manifests will pin the current code, inputs, and delivered instructions.
